100% Tuition Subsidy

DepEd Senior High School Voucher Program

Official guidelines on financial subsidies and zero-tuition walk-in enrollment for incoming Grade 11 & Grade 12 students.

100% Free Tuition

Public JHS Completers

All Grade 10 completers from Department of Education (DepEd) public junior high schools are automatically qualified for the 100% Full Voucher Subsidy. No top-up tuition fees required!

ESC & QVR Grantees

Private JHS Voucher Grantees

Grade 10 completers from private junior high schools who are Education Service Contracting (ESC) grantees or Qualified Voucher Recipients (QVR) receive full or substantial tuition subsidy upon presenting their certificate.

No Entrance Exam

Zero Admission & Exam Fees

DCSA does not charge any entrance examination fee. Qualified applicants can directly walk in during regular office hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM) with complete documentary credentials for immediate evaluation.

Step-by-Step Guide

Walk-In Admission & Enrollment Procedure

Follow these simple steps when visiting any DCSA campus branch for fast and hassle-free processing.

Step 1

Campus Arrival & Inquiry

Visit the Admissions Office at your chosen campus branch during official office hours (Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM). Fill out the Admission Inquiry Slip at the desk.

Step 2

Document Submission

Submit your Form 138 (Report Card), Certificate of Good Moral Character, and PSA Birth Certificate to the admissions evaluator for credential verification.

Step 3

Voucher & Strand Evaluation

For Senior High School, present your DepEd Voucher or ESC Certificate for 100% tuition subsidy processing. College applicants undergo program advising.

Step 4

Sectioning & Confirmation

Receive your official registration form, assigned class schedule, uniform specifications, and orientation guide. Your admission is officially confirmed!

Walk-In Checklist

Requirements to Bring for Walk-In Enrollment

Please bring the following original and photocopy documents when visiting any DCSA campus branch.

Requirement 1

Report Card / Form 138

Original Grade 10 / Grade 12 Report Card (Form 138) or Official Transcript of Records (Form 137).

Requirement 2

Good Moral Certificate

Certificate of Good Moral Character issued by your previous school principal or guidance office.

Requirement 3

PSA Birth Certificate

Photocopy of Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Birth Certificate (with original presented for verification).

Requirement 4

2x2 ID Pictures

2 copies of recent 2x2 ID pictures in formal attire with white background and applicant's name tag.
