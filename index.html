Student Life & Standards

Official School Uniform Reference

Visual guide and dress code policy for all students of Datamex College of St. Adeline.

Campus Dress Code & ID Policy

All students are required to wear their complete official school uniform together with their valid DCSA Student ID & Lanyard upon entering the campus premises.

Female Uniform: White uniform blouse with official school necktie/scarf, maroon skirt, white knee-high socks, and black shoes.
Male Uniform: White uniform polo shirt with official school necktie, maroon trousers, and black leather shoes.
Uniform Availability: Complete uniform sets, patches, and accessories are available at the campus bookstore and administrative office.
Student Welfare & Campus Security

Safe, Protected & Caring Campus Community

At Datamex College of St. Adeline, the safety, health, and holistic well-being of every learner are our highest priorities. Parents can be confident that our campus community is vigilant, proactive, and genuinely caring.

S.Y. 2026–2027 Valenzuela Branch

Anti-Bullying & Child Protection Committee

"To promote a safe, respectful, and inclusive school environment where every learner is protected, valued, and empowered. One Datamex, One Community, Zero Bullying."

  • Zero-Tolerance Bullying Policy: Strict prevention and prohibition against all forms of bullying, cyberbullying, harassment, and intimidation.
  • Dedicated Welfare Personnel: On-site Registered Guidance Counselor, Child Protection Officers, Prefect of Discipline, and First Aiders.
  • Student & Parent Representation: Active representation from student council presidents across academic strands and designated Parents' Representatives.
Campus Clinic Guidelines

When Should You Stay Home? Health Protocol

"Your health matters. Let's stop the spread and keep our community safe." Clear clinical standards for attendance, self-care, and campus health monitoring.

  • Clear Attendance Benchmarks: 'OK for School' when symptom-free and energetic; 'Must Go Home / Rest' when experiencing fever (over 38°C), contagious rashes, or persistent cough.
  • Campus Clinic Nurse Support: On-duty medical professionals available to evaluate students, administer first aid, and monitor well-being.
  • Prompt Parent & Guardian Coordination: Immediate communication with families if a student requires pickup or medical recovery at home.
Campus Security Drill

Active Threat Drill: Safety Guidelines

"Stay Informed. Be Prepared. Protect Yourself and Others." Proactive emergency response training ensuring institutional security and campus vigilance.

  • RUN • HIDE • FIGHT Protocols: Standardized institutional emergency drill training practiced across staff, faculty, and student bodies.
  • Direct Law Enforcement Coordination: Immediate emergency contact with Valenzuela City Police Station (8352 4000) and Unified 911.
  • Safety, Unity & Preparedness: Well-trained safety officers and faculty marshals ready to protect and lead students during crises.
Air Quality Advisory

Haze Alert: Health Protection Advisory

"Stay Informed. Take Caution. Protect Your Health. Safety First!" Practical preventive measures during smog, smoke, and poor air quality events.

  • 4 Core Preventive Steps: 01 Stay Indoors, 02 Wear N95 Face Masks, 03 Seal Room Openings with Damp Cloths, 04 Safe Commuting with Headlights/Fog Lights.
  • Support for Respiratory Conditions: Focused care and medical guidance for students with asthma or environmental sensitivities.
  • Safe & Ventilated Learning Spaces: Regulated airflow and secure indoor environments throughout all classrooms and training labs.
