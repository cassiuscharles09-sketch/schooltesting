## 1. INTRODUCTION

The rapid transformation of modern educational technology has highlighted the critical importance of accessible, centralized, and transparent digital information systems in academic institutions. Developed as a major requirement for the course Web System Technologies, the **DATAMEX College of Saint Adeline (DCSA) Web-Based Information System** is an institutional public showcase platform designed to serve prospective students, parents, transferees, and campus visitors across its four official branches located in Fairview, Caloocan, Valenzuela, and Meycauayan. The platform provides a digital gateway that showcases Commission on Higher Education (CHED) accredited collegiate degrees, Department of Education (DepEd) recognized Senior High School strands, state-of-the-art laboratory facilities, and official school uniform dress code guidelines in one unified environment.

Prior to the conceptualization and deployment of this web system, prospective applicants and parents encountered persistent challenges in obtaining accurate, timely, and centralized school information. Essential details regarding academic curricula, branch landmarks, office operating schedules, and documentary admission prerequisites were dispersed across fragmented physical bulletin boards and informal social media announcements. This lack of a structured information channel frequently resulted in unprepared walk-in applicants arriving without mandatory credentials, such as Form 138 report cards, Certificates of Good Moral Character, and PSA Birth Certificates. The DCSA Web-Based Information System was therefore developed to eliminate multi-branch ambiguity, reduce administrative bottlenecks at campus registrar desks, and empower families with clear, 24/7 accessible pre-enrollment guidelines and 100% DepEd Voucher subsidy information before they visit any campus in person.

Built exclusively using standards-compliant **Semantic HTML5** and modern **CSS3**, the platform achieves high-performance responsiveness, intuitive navigation, and visual aesthetics without relying on complex backend frameworks or dynamic database engines (**100% Pure HTML5 & CSS3**). HTML5 establishes a structured document hierarchy with built-in client-side form validation for visitor inquiries, while CSS3 leverages Flexbox, Grid, CSS Custom Properties, and media queries to ensure smooth visual rendering across desktop monitors, tablets, and smartphones. Furthermore, the implementation of pure CSS interactive techniques, including the checkbox toggle hack for mobile navigation drawers and seamless section jumping, guarantees maximum security, zero loading latency, and consistent cross-browser compatibility across all modern web browsers.

## 2. PROJECT OVERVIEW

### 2.1 System Name
DATAMEX College of Saint Adeline (DCSA) Web-Based Information System

### 2.2 System Description
The DATAMEX College of Saint Adeline (DCSA) Web-Based Information System is a responsive web platform designed to serve as the institution's primary informational hub for prospective students, parents, and visitors. The system provides structured academic details, showcasing Senior High School strands, College degree programs, campus facilities, and industry accreditations across all four DCSA campuses. It assists prospective enrollees by presenting clear walk-in admission guidelines, documentary requirement checklists, branch locations, and DepEd voucher subsidy information during official school office hours from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Furthermore, the system serves as a centralized source for official uniform dress code references, facility overviews, and direct inquiry channels. Designed for seamless access on both desktop and mobile devices, the web system improves information transparency and enables applicants to prepare all required documents before personally visiting their chosen campus branch.

### 2.3 Problem Statement
Prior to the implementation of the system, DATAMEX College of Saint Adeline (DCSA) faced difficulties in delivering centralized information to prospective students, parents, and visitors across its four campus branches. Prospective applicants frequently arrived at the school unprepared or missing essential admission documents, resulting in delayed processing and unnecessary repeated walk-in visits. Furthermore, the lack of a unified digital platform created confusion regarding specific branch locations, active contact numbers, academic program offerings, and the coverage of the 100% DepEd Senior High School Voucher subsidy. Important institutional guidelines, uniform dress code standards, and office schedules were also scattered across informal channels and physical bulletin boards, making them difficult to access reliably. Consequently, front-desk staff spent substantial office hours manually answering repetitive basic inquiries instead of focusing on processing on-site walk-in applications and student records.

### 2.4 Proposed Solution
To address the identified challenges, the DATAMEX College of Saint Adeline (DCSA) Web-Based Information System was developed to serve as a centralized digital platform that delivers complete and accessible institutional information. The system directly resolves the issue of unprepared walk-in applicants by presenting comprehensive admission guidelines, documentary requirement checklists, and official office hours from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, ensuring students prepare all necessary records prior to their campus visit. It eliminates multi-branch confusion by organizing verified contact directories and location details for all four campuses, while clearly showcasing available Senior High School strands, College degree programs, and 100% DepEd Voucher subsidy coverage. Additionally, the platform centralizes facility overviews and student uniform dress code references in one verified digital location. Through its built-in inquiry channel and accessible information structure, the web system significantly reduces repetitive front-desk questions, allowing campus personnel to process on-site walk-in admissions more efficiently.

## 3. PROJECT GOAL AND OBJECTIVES

### 3.1 Project Goal
The main goal of this project is to develop a responsive, web-based Information and Public Showcase System for Datamex College of Saint Adeline (DCSA) using pure HTML5 and CSS3 that provides students, parents, prospective applicants, and visitors with easy, centralized access to vital institutional information, such as academic programs, walk-in admission guidelines, campus directories, facilities, and uniform standards across all four DCSA branches.

### 3.2 Specific Objectives
The project is designed to accomplish the following specific objectives:
- To develop a web-based informational platform that presents the complete curricula and academic career pathways for Commission on Higher Education (CHED) accredited collegiate programs (BSIT, BSHM, BSBA, ACT) and Department of Education (DepEd) recognized Senior High School strands (STEM, ABM, HUMSS, TVL-ICT, TVL-HE).
- To provide users with comprehensive walk-in admission guidelines and documentary checklists, specifically detailing the submission of Form 138 report cards, Good Moral certificates, PSA birth certificates, and 100% DepEd Voucher subsidy benefits.
- To implement pure HTML5 and CSS3 interactive features, such as a responsive mobile navigation drawer utilizing the CSS checkbox toggle technique, smooth section jumping, and modern 16:9 alternating widescreen facility layouts without relying on JavaScript or external script libraries.
- To provide appropriate HTML5 form input validation for client-side public inquiries by applying required attributes and specific input types across the contact interface.
- To organize multi-campus branch information by consolidating verified addresses, nearby landmarks, direct Google Maps navigation links, and official Facebook page buttons for the Fairview, Caloocan, Valenzuela, and Meycauayan campuses.
- To evaluate and test the overall system performance, ensuring visual consistency, rapid page load speed, cross-browser compatibility, and seamless responsiveness across diverse screen resolutions.

## 4. PROJECT SCOPE

The system encompasses a comprehensive 5-page public viewing platform covering institutional information for Datamex College of Saint Adeline:

- **Functions Available:**
  - Full-featured multi-page site navigation with responsive mobile menu toggle.
  - Interactive quick-portal navigation linking to admissions, programs, facilities, and inquiries.
  - Interactive multi-campus location directory with one-click Google Maps and Facebook links.
  - Client-side public inquiry form with HTML5 field validation.
  - Visual dress code policy and official uniform photo showcase.
  - Modern 16:9 alternating split facility presentation.
- **Users Who Can Access the System:**
  - Prospective Students (Senior High School and College).
  - Parents and Guardians.
  - Current Students and Transferees.
  - General Public and Campus Visitors.
  - School Administrative and Admissions Staff.
- **Data Handled:**
  - Academic program descriptions, career pathways, and accreditation details.
  - Institutional history, philosophy, mission, vision, and core values (DCSA: Discipline, Competence, Service, Achievement).
  - Walk-in documentary checklist and voucher subsidy guidelines.
  - Campus addresses, landmarks, hotlines, emails, and operating hours.
  - Client-side inquiry submissions (Name, Email, Subject, Message).
- **Pages / Modules:**
  1. `index.html` – Home & Institutional Overview
  2. `about.html` – About DCSA, Campus Facilities (16:9 Split Layout), Mission & Vision
  3. `academics.html` – Undergraduate Degree Programs & Senior High School Strands
  4. `admissions.html` – 4 Campus Branches, Maps, and Walk-in Requirements Checklist
  5. `contact.html` – Contact Directory, Campus Hotlines, Office Hours, and Inquiry Form
- **HTML/CSS Functions Implemented:**
  - Semantic HTML5 structure (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`).
  - CSS Flexbox and CSS Grid layout engines.
  - CSS Custom Properties (Variables) for consistent school branding palette.
  - CSS Checkbox Navigation Toggle Hack (`.menu-toggle:checked ~ .nav-links`) for 100% JS-free mobile menus.
  - CSS Media Queries for adaptive layout across mobile (320px–640px), tablet (768px), and desktop (1024px+).
  - CSS `aspect-ratio: 16 / 9` with `object-fit: cover` for responsive facility photos.
  - CSS `scroll-behavior: smooth` for smooth section jumping.

### 4.1 Included Features:
- **Hero Banner with Core Institutional Statistics:** Displays 15+ Years of Excellence, 100% DepEd Voucher Subsidy, and 4 Metro Campuses.
- **Official Student Uniform & Dress Code Guide:** Inset photograph and detailed guidelines for female and male uniform standards and ID lanyard policies.
- **Widescreen Facilities Showcase (16:9 Split Layout):** Alternating left/right media blocks highlighting Air-Conditioned Computer Labs, Commercial Culinary & Bar Kitchen, and E-Library & Multimedia Center.

## 5. PROJECT LIMITATIONS

- **Web Browser Environment:** The system is designed to run in standard web browsers (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Opera) and is not packaged as a native mobile application (.apk / .ipa).
- **Pure Informational / Viewing Platform:** The system does not include dynamic financial payment gateways (no GCash, credit card, or bank API integrations) or online tuition billing.
- **No Backend Database or User Accounts:** The platform operates as a static client-side web system (zero SQL/database drivers, zero user authentication logins, zero dynamic student/faculty portals).
- **In-Person Walk-In Admissions:** Formal student enrollment, credential verification, and physical document submission must be finalized on-site at the campus admissions office.
- **Internet Dependency for Remote Assets:** Accessing external Google Web Fonts (`Poppins`) and external Google Maps links requires an active internet connection.

### 9.2 System Flow
1. **Landing & Exploration:** The user opens the web application and lands on `index.html`, where they are greeted by the institution's hero banner, core achievements, and uniform dress code reference.
2. **Program & Facility Discovery:** The user navigates via the top header or quick portals to `about.html` to review school history and explore 16:9 widescreen facilities, or visits `academics.html` to review collegiate degrees and SHS strands.
3. **Admissions Preparation:** The user accesses `admissions.html` to find the nearest campus branch (Fairview, Caloocan, Valenzuela, or Meycauayan), review landmarks, open Google Maps for directions, and prepare required admission documents based on the walk-in checklist.
4. **Direct Inquiry / Contact:** If the user has specific inquiries, they navigate to `contact.html` to obtain office hotlines, operating schedules, or submit a message via the inquiry form.
